Kunwoong Kim

Hi! I’m a postdoctoral researcher in KAIST AI, working with Jinwoo Shin. Before that, I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics at Seoul National University, advised by Yongdai Kim. My research brings together harnessing LLMs and AI agents efficiently and reliably, foundational questions in machine learning, and applications of AI across diverse domains, including science.
